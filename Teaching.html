Teaching experience Anthony Massidda

Teaching

Alongside research, I have taught Mathematics and Physics for about ten years, at university, high-school and higher technical institute level, to students with very different backgrounds: physicists, mathematicians, chemists, engineers, pharmacy and optometry students, and high-school students.

My approach combines mathematical rigour with physical intuition. I adapt explanations to each student's preparation while keeping high standards, and I put particular emphasis on analytical reasoning, independent problem solving, and on connecting abstract concepts with concrete applications.
